03 / HOW TO PARTICIPATE

Make a moment.
Make it matter.

No professional camera or perfect script needed. Just your family, a diya and a thought worth sharing.

01

Light a diya together

Invite your parents or family members and light a traditional diya safely.

02

Capture your moment

Take a clear photo or record a short video. Speak naturally in Hindi or English.

03

Share your promise

Tell us what your diya represents and upload your photo or video below.

04 / YOUR VIDEO, YOUR VOICE

Some ideas to
get you started.

Use these as inspiration, not a script to memorise. Make the message your own.

A
Begin with the diya

Show the diya being lit. Let the flame appear clearly in the frame.

B
Say your promise

“This diya is for a cleaner India. I promise not to throw waste into our rivers.”

C
End with an invitation

“What will your diya stand for? Let us make this festival meaningful together.”

RECOMMENDED: 20–60 SECONDS · VERTICAL OR HORIZONTAL · CLEAR AUDIO
